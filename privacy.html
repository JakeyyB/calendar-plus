Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Calendar Plus is a personal browser extension for Google Calendar. This page explains what data it touches and what happens to it.

Summary

Calendar Plus runs entirely inside your own browser. It has no server of its own. It does not collect, store, sell or share your data with anyone.

Google account data

When you use double-click editing, Calendar Plus asks Google for permission to view and edit events on your calendars (the calendar.events permission). It uses that permission for exactly two things:

  • to read the single event you double-clicked, so its title, time and description can be shown in the edit box;
  • to save the changes you make to that event when you press Save.

These requests go directly from your browser to Google's Calendar API. Event data is shown on your screen and is not copied, logged or sent anywhere else.

What is stored

  • Your settings (for example your active hours and highlight colour) are kept in your browser's extension storage, which your browser may sync between your own devices if you have its sync feature switched on.
  • The Google sign-in token is kept in your browser for the current browsing session only and is discarded when the browser closes.

Calendar Plus itself stores nothing anywhere else.

What is not done

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No data is transferred to third parties.
  • No data is used for any purpose other than the features described above.

Calendar Plus's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove Calendar Plus's access at any time from your Google Account under Third-party apps and services, or by removing the extension from your browser.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.

Calendar Plus is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google.

Calendar Plus

A small personal browser extension that makes Google Calendar quicker to use.

What it does

  • Arrow-key navigation – the left and right arrow keys move to the previous or next day, week or month, depending on the view that is open.
  • Today highlight – today's column is lightly shaded so it is easy to spot.
  • Active hours – day and week views show only the hours you choose, for example 06:00 to midnight.
  • Double-click to edit – double-click an event to change its title, time or description without leaving the calendar.

How it uses your Google account

The first three features only change how the Google Calendar page looks and behaves in your browser. Double-click editing asks for permission to view and edit events on your calendars, so that it can load the event you double-clicked and save the change you make to it. It does nothing else with that access.

See the privacy policy for the details.

Availability

Calendar Plus is a private, personal project. It is not listed in any extension store and is not offered to the public.
